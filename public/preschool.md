# Montessori Pre-School in Choueifat

The Bloom — Children’s House at La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds is a Montessori pre-school for ages 3–6 in Choueifat.

## What children experience

Mixed-age classroom, Montessori materials, practical life, language and mathematics, and uninterrupted work cycles. Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
