# Montessori Nursery & Preschool FAQs

## What is the Montessori method?

Montessori is an educational approach built around how children naturally develop. In a prepared classroom, children choose hands-on work, practise at their own pace, and build independence with a trained Guide who observes rather than lectures.

## What age groups does Montessori Minds serve?

Infant Community (birth–18 months), Toddler Community (18 months–3 years), and Children’s House (3–6 years).

## Where is the campus?

Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat, Mount Lebanon, Lebanon — [La Casa Verde](https://www.montessorimindslb.com/la-casa-verde).

## What are school hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

[Book a visit](https://www.montessorimindslb.com/book-a-tour), then continue through [admissions](https://www.montessorimindslb.com/admission). Tuition is shared in person.
