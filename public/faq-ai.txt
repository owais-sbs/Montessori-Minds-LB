Q: What is La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds?
A: La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds is a Montessori preschool, nursery and school on Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat, Mount Lebanon, Lebanon. It offers Infant Community (birth–18 months), Toddler Community (18 months–3 years) and Children’s House (ages 3–6). Hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.
URL: https://www.montessorimindslb.com/la-casa-verde

---

Q: What age groups do you serve?
A: Infancy through age six: Infant Community / Nido (birth–18 months), Toddler Community (18 months–3 years), and Children’s House pre-school (3–6 years). Placement is confirmed during a campus visit.
URL: https://www.montessorimindslb.com/programs

Q: What is the Montessori method here?
A: Children choose hands-on work in a prepared classroom and progress at their own pace with a Guide who observes rather than lectures.
