# Montessori Nursery in Choueifat

La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds offers Montessori nursery pathways for infants and toddlers on Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat.

## Age groups

- The Nest — Infant Community (Nido): birth–18 months
- The Sprout — Toddler Community: 18 months–3 years

Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.

Continue into [Montessori pre-school](https://www.montessorimindslb.com/preschool) on the same campus, or [book a visit](https://www.montessorimindslb.com/book-a-tour).
