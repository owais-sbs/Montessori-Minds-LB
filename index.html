Montessori School, Pre-School & Nursery in Choueifat

Welcome to La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds — a Montessori school in Choueifat, Lebanon offering nurturing nursery and pre-school pathways. We support independence, curiosity, and confidence through authentic Montessori education for infants, toddlers, and young children.

Montessori Education at Montessori Minds

Families seeking Montessori education in Lebanon — including those coming from the greater Beirut and Baabda areas — find a calm prepared environment in Choueifat rooted in nature, play, and hands-on discovery. Our guides honor each child’s pace while building a genuine partnership with families.

Nursery and Pre-School Programs

Our Montessori nursery pathways include The Nest for infants and The Sprout for toddlers. The Bloom offers Montessori pre-school for ages 3–6. Explore each pathway on our programs page.
