La Casa Verde | Montessori Preschool in Choueifat, Lebanon

Welcome to La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds — a nature-inspired Montessori preschool in Choueifat, Lebanon. We nurture independence, curiosity, and confidence through authentic Montessori education for infants, toddlers, and preschoolers.

Why Families Choose Our Montessori Preschool

Choosing a preschool is a big decision. At La Casa Verde in Choueifat, children learn in a prepared environment rooted in nature, play, and hands-on discovery. Our guides honor each child’s individual growth while building a true partnership with families.

Our Montessori Programs

We offer three developmentally aligned programs: The Nest for infants (0–18 months), The Sprout for toddlers (18 months–3 years), and The Bloom for preschoolers (3–6 years). Each classroom supports independence, concentration, and joyful learning.

A Prepared Environment in Choueifat

Our Montessori classrooms and outdoor spaces help children move freely, choose purposeful work, and connect with the natural world. Practical life, sensory learning, grace and courtesy, and outdoor exploration are part of every day at La Casa Verde.

Visit La Casa Verde

The best way to experience our Montessori preschool in Lebanon is to visit. Start an application or book a tour to see our school on Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat.
