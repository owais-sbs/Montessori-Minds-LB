Montessori Minds — Montessori school in Choueifat

La Casa Verde by Montessori Minds is a Montessori school on Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat, Lebanon. We run Infant Community (birth–18 months), Toddler Community (18 months–3 years) and Children’s House (ages 3–6). Campus hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM. Phone +961 71 007 150. Email hello@montessorimindslb.com.

Our Montessori programs

  • Infant Community (Nido)

    Birth–18 months — secure care, sensory exploration, responsive language.

  • Toddler Community

    18 months–3 years — practical life, movement and early independence.

  • Children’s House

    Ages 3–6 — Montessori pre-school with mixed ages and long work cycles.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is the Montessori method?

    Children choose hands-on work in a prepared classroom and progress at their own pace with a trained Guide who observes rather than lectures.

  • Where are you located?

    Saida Old Road, Al Omara District, Choueifat, Mount Lebanon, Lebanon.

  • How do I enroll?

    Book a campus visit, complete an application, then plan a gradual settling-in period with our team.
