Nursery and Pre-School Programs

Our Montessori nursery pathways include The Nest for infants and The Sprout for toddlers — nurturing early years care with sensory-rich exploration. The Bloom offers Montessori pre-school for ages 3–6, with hands-on materials, mixed-age community, and growing focus. Together, nursery and pre-school create continuity for families who want one trusted Montessori school community in Choueifat. Whether you are comparing preschool options in Choueifat or searching for a nursery school that respects toddlers’ need to move and try, our pathways are designed around real developmental stages — not busywork.
